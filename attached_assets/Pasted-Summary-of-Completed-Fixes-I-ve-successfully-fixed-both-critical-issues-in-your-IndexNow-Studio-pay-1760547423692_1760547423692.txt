1. ✅ Midtrans Payment Amount Conversion Bug - FIXED
The Problem:

Your database stores prices in cents (e.g., 80000 = $800.00)
The Midtrans service was treating these as dollars, so 80000 → $80,000.00
After currency conversion: 80,000 × 15,800 IDR/USD = 1.26 billion IDR (exceeds Midtrans limit!)
Should have been: 800 × 15,800 = 12.6 million IDR ✓
The Fix:
Added smart conversion logic in all 4 payment methods:

// If amount >= 100 → stored in cents, divide by 100 to get dollars
const amountInDollars = amount >= 100 ? amount / 100 : amount
This correctly handles all package prices:

$1 package: 100 cents ÷ 100 = $1 ✓
$50 package: 5,000 cents ÷ 100 = $50 ✓
$100 package: 10,000 cents ÷ 100 = $100 ✓
$800 package: 80,000 cents ÷ 100 = $800 ✓
